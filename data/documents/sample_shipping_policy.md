# Northstar Outfitters — Shipping Policy

> Demo document only. Northstar Outfitters is fictional.

## Processing

Orders placed before 2:00 PM Pakistan Standard Time on business days are usually processed within one business day. Orders placed after that time, on weekends, or on public holidays are processed on the next business day.

## Delivery Estimates

Standard delivery within Pakistan typically takes 3–5 business days after dispatch. Express delivery typically takes 1–2 business days after dispatch. Delivery estimates are not guarantees and may be affected by weather, carrier disruptions, or remote-area delivery requirements.

## Tracking and Address Changes

Customers receive a tracking link by email after an order is dispatched. Address changes can be requested before dispatch by contacting Support. Once dispatched, an address change cannot be guaranteed.

## Lost Packages

If tracking has not updated for 7 business days, customers should contact Support with their order number. Northstar Outfitters will investigate with the carrier and may issue a replacement or refund after the investigation is complete.
