# Northstar Outfitters — Returns and Refunds Policy

> Demo document only. Northstar Outfitters is fictional.

## Eligibility

Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days of delivery. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase.

Final-sale items, gift cards, and personalized products cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged or incorrect.

## Refunds

After the returned item passes inspection, a refund is issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days. Original shipping charges are not refundable unless Northstar Outfitters made an error.

## Damaged or Incorrect Orders

Customers should contact Support within 7 days of delivery and include their order number plus photographs of the issue. Northstar Outfitters will provide a replacement or a full refund, including standard shipping costs.

## Exchanges

Exchanges depend on inventory availability. Customers should submit a return request and place a new order for the preferred item.
