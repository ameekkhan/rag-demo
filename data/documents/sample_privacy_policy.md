# Northstar Outfitters — Privacy Notice

> Demo document only. Northstar Outfitters is fictional and this is not legal advice.

## Information Collected

We collect account details, contact information, delivery addresses, order history, and customer-support communications. Payment card details are processed by our payment provider and are not stored by Northstar Outfitters.

## How We Use Information

We use personal information to fulfil orders, provide customer support, prevent fraud, improve our services, and send marketing messages where consent is provided. Customers can opt out of marketing messages by using the unsubscribe link in an email.

## Retention and Security

We retain order records for seven years to meet accounting and tax obligations. We use access controls and encryption in transit to help protect personal information.

## Privacy Requests

To request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, contact privacy@northstar.example. We respond to verified requests within 30 days, subject to applicable legal requirements.
